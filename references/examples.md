# Examples

## Simple Prompt

Input:

一只狐狸站在雪山上，很孤独，电影感。

Output direction:

Add:
- visible behavior
- environment
- lighting
- camera
- sound
- continuity

Do not add:
- unrelated story
- dialogue
- invented identity

## Emotion

Convert:

"紧张"

into observable actions:

- breathing
- eye movement
- posture
- hand movement
